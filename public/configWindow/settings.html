Text-generation parameters

Court Historian

How often the court historian writes a chronicle entry. Disabled turns off automatic generation.
Maximum total characters of raw source material sent to the model. Sources exceeding this limit are summarized.
Maximum tokens for the generated chronicle entry.

Save-game facts (experimental)

Read a structured snapshot of your save game (family, realm, wars, court) and use it as verified facts. Debug-mode saves are plain text; normal-mode saves are binary and parsed via melt (requires a CK3 version covered by the built-in token table). When off, the app never touches save files.
Allow launching the bundled save-parser helper on the snapshot file. Requires the master switch above.
Inject the verified save-game facts into conversation prompts as a bounded game_facts block. Requires the two switches above.
Expose anonymous save-fact diagnostics (status codes and category counts only) to this settings window. 

            

            

              
              The snapshot is read from a save file this mod writes (save games/votc_context.ck3). It never leaves your machine; only derived fact lines are sent to your configured LLM provider as part of prompts. All of this stays off unless you enable it here.
